> MOCK DATA for testing the agent team. Invented people, invented quotes. Not real research.

- Participant: P3, financial crime team lead, 12 years in role, mid-size investment bank
- Date: 2026-10-08, 35 minutes, video call
- Interviewer: Kevin

## Notes

Reviews suspicious activity report drafts from five investigators before they are filed. Reviews about 15 per week, more near quarter end.

Biggest time cost is sending drafts back for unclear reasoning. Roughly one in three goes back at least once.

> "The facts are usually right. What's missing is why they think it's suspicious. I should not have to guess."

Uses a checklist from a policy document. It is a PDF, so she copies items into an email when sending feedback.

> "I wrote the same feedback sentence eleven times last month. There should be a way to save that."

Cares about an audit trail: who changed what, and when. Said the regulator once asked for the reasoning behind a late filing and it took two days to reconstruct.

> "Two days, for a question I should be able to answer in two minutes."

Worries about anything automated that writes narrative text, unless she can see exactly what it was based on.

> "I'm fine with help. I'm not fine with a black box that I sign my name under."

## Observations
- Keeps a private spreadsheet of common feedback phrases.
- Looked up a filing deadline in a separate calendar instead of the case tool.
- Asked whether the product would show which statements in a narrative were supported by evidence.
