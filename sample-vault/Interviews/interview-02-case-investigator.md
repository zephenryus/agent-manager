- Date: 2026-10-07, 50 minutes, in person

## Notes

Handles escalated cases, usually 8 to 12 open at a time. Cases can stay open for weeks while waiting on documents from the business.

Builds a timeline of events by hand in a Word document, then pastes it into the case narrative. Says the timeline is "the real work."

> "The case tool stores the facts. It doesn't help me tell the story. I rebuild the story every time."

Counterparty relationships are the hardest part. Draws link diagrams on paper, then photographs them to attach to the case.

> "If two customers share an address and a director, I can see it. Getting that into the file in a way the reviewer believes takes an hour."

Gets frustrated when a case is reassigned. The notes from the previous investigator are there but "you can't tell which ones they trusted."

> "I never know if the last person checked the source or just copied it."

Has stopped using the tool's comment field because comments are not searchable.

## Observations
- Had a notepad file open called "TODO cases" beside the tool.
